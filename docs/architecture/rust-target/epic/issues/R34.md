Preserve retention and privacy obligations with verifiable, versioned rule evidence.

## Scope

- Port retention calculations, legal holds, discovery/forget and deletion authorization

- Port compliance/regulatory coverage reports, legal-source/rule metadata and immutable audit visibility

- [ ] Eligible forgetting preserves mandatory financial/audit evidence and records the actual effect

- [ ] Rule/source IDs and versions validate consistently; no external legal claim is invented by a Rust port
