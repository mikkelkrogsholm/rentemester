# Native service company mutations

Compose existing real manual post/reversal and durable receipts for service principals on admitted canonical control/company connections. No product opener or HTTP/CLI/MCP writer is enabled. Physical identity/lifetime/manifest and legacy writer admission, outer actor-policy/confirmation/backup and full platform delivery remain required.

One synchronous call has two separate commits. First validate the typed envelope, workspace/key binding, trusted selected company and both connection preflights. Under control BEGIN IMMEDIATE, observe current exact live credential/registered service subject/workspace access and explicit company membership; authorize ledger.post before charging one quota/rate attempt. Commit usage and require known success. Then reacquire control BEGIN IMMEDIATE, reobserve the same exact credential ID and stable subject using the same key hash and a fresh clock, and reauthorize current company access. This second observation validates metadata but does not consume/refill again or demand remaining/rate headroom already spent by the first phase. Hold this control writer through the company commit.

A revoke/disable/rotation/expiry/membership change between phases denies company access while keeping the known charged attempt. Phase2 current authority precedes any protected receipt lookup. Workspace owner alone grants no company membership. Services need registered/live credential and active workspace/company membership, without human email/MFA/session requirements. The typed credential observation is transient trusted storage evidence, never deserialized/serialized authority or a display JSON projection.

Real post/reversal and receipt remain atomic in the company transaction. Receipt identity is ServiceAccount plus stable service subject, never credential ID/hash/actor/path. A new valid key on the same subject can replay; a revoked old key cannot. Runtime supplies actor user:subject via service-principal; the existing reversal policy retains its verified original import marker for a historical documentless reversal. Client payload cannot control identity or attribution. Every valid new attempt, including receipt replay/conflict and later domain refusal, can consume quota again. No refund, no exactly-once logical-command quota and no cross-database atomic quota+business claim.

Outcomes report usage NotCommitted/OutcomeUnknown/Committed separately from business NotAttempted/NotCommitted/OutcomeUnknown/Committed and connection reuse flags. Only known usage commit permits company attempt; only known company commit permits successful result/receipt disclosure. Company failure never implies usage rollback. Unknown usage commit retires control and makes no company attempt. Known company success survives final control cleanup failure. Business Committed describes the company transaction, including a diagnostic-only receipt conflict; callers must inspect result.ok and must not treat stage alone as domain success. No credential secret/hash/ID is serialized, audited or logged by this orchestration.

Preserve existing /api/me quota/refill/inactivity boundaries and strict metadata corrections, including its documented native exact-window increment correction: the installed source CAS recursively retries at equality, so frozen-clock source verification explicitly excludes that single branch and native effects are asserted directly. Source provider charges before later company authorization; this native company protocol deliberately denies known unauthorized company selection before charge, then charges later refusals. That ordering is explicit native semantics, not claimed source cross-database parity. Existing human context/post/reversal behavior must remain unchanged.

Qualify real synthetic WAL/DELETE control and company databases, source-issued credentials/current source authorization observations and actual native post/reversal. Cover last remaining/rate slot, charged domain refusals/replays, stable subject rotation, revoked authority, between-phase revocation/expiry, real commit-busy uncertainty, killed writers and concurrent quota/same-key attempts. Compare complete company effects against actual source journals and independently verify source hashes/audits; scoped readiness and passing helpers alone do not complete R13/R14 or the epic.

Recovery is explicit; this primitive performs no automatic retries:
